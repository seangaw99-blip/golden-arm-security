Website notices

Privacy Policy & Website Notices

How this website handles inquiry information, serviceability maps, illustrative media, technology evidence, careers information, and credential references.

Clear details, kept in one place.

This page consolidates the website notices that previously appeared repeatedly beside imagery, maps, forms, and route content. Short notices remain beside actions when they affect what a visitor should do.

01 · Inquiries

Inquiry forms and email handoff

The inquiry forms do not send or store completed form details through this website. When JavaScript is available, the page prepares a draft in the visitor’s email app. The visitor reviews the draft and chooses whether to send it. If sent, the visitor’s email provider and Golden Arm’s email systems handle the message for inquiry follow-up.

Do not include passwords, confidential floor plans, camera layouts, access codes, or sensitive security procedures. If no email app opens, contact admin@goldenarmsecurity.ph or use the telephone details on the Contact page.

02 · Coverage

Representative assessment areas

Map markers are inquiry starting points. They do not identify Golden Arm branches, clients, deployments, personnel posts, or operating locations, and they do not guarantee current availability. Golden Arm confirms serviceability after reviewing the exact site, timing, staffing, mobilization, supervision, and coordination needs.

03 · Media

Illustrative and generated media

Some website images are planning or atmospheric visualizations. They support the presentation of services, environments, and operating concepts; they are not evidence of a named client, exact site, live post, deployment, employee, vacancy, control room, or operating record. Documentary photographs and product-interface evidence are identified through their context and accessible descriptions.

04 · Technology

Technology evidence

Technology imagery uses publication-redacted interface evidence and excludes confidential operational records. The Guard App link opens Golden Arm’s separate login service; it is not a public download, registration, feature-availability, or performance guarantee.

05 · Careers

Role families and current openings

The Careers page describes broad role families and employee programs. It is not a live vacancy list. Current openings, requirements, and application handling are controlled by Golden Arm’s official Vantier application service.

06 · Credentials

Verification and status

Credential categories and historical references explain the kinds of records that may support an assessment. They do not assert an unverified current status, certification, award, endorsement, or applicability to a particular engagement. Current evidence and applicability must be confirmed directly with Golden Arm.

07 · Operations

Planning diagrams and operating models

Diagrams, frameworks, and planning visuals explain a process or operating concept. They are not live operational reports, site records, post orders, client documents, or proof of a particular deployment.

08 · Contact

Questions about these notices

For questions about the website, inquiry handling, or these notices, email admin@goldenarmsecurity.ph or use the details on the Contact page.

This page describes the behavior and boundaries of the current website. It does not claim practices, systems, certifications, or legal conclusions that have not been confirmed by Golden Arm.
