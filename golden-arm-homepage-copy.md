## The format (for the remaining pages)

Every line must pass three gates:

1. **Voice** — plain over corporate; confident, not defensive (no "really/actually/truly"); concrete over abstract; second person; active verbs; fixed vocabulary (guard, post, shift, check-in, coverage).
2. **Readability** — body copy at grade 5 or below; sentences mostly under 12 words; common words; one idea each. Headlines: plain and short (single lines score erratically, so judge the words, not the number).
3. **SEO** — target keyword appears naturally in the H1, first sentence, and at least one H2; descriptive headers; an internal link where it fits; no stuffing.

**Loop, per page:** Step 0 — lock the SEO brief (keyword, title, meta, header outline). Steps 1…n — per section: current copy → issues → two scored options → pick → lock. Final — assemble clean copy + SEO checklist.

**Page order to work through:** Home ✅ → Services ✅ → About ✅ → Industries ✅ → Technology ✅ → Guard App page ✅ → Careers ✅ → Contact ✅ → FAQ ✅ → utility pages (Coverage, Privacy).
