Guard App

See your site in real time.

01 · Verified clock-in

Guards clock in with a selfie at your site, geofenced to the post. You get proof of who's on, and when.

Guards check in and out by scanning a QR code tied to their exact post. Every check-in is stamped with the time and place—so you always know a real guard is on duty, right where they should be.

02 · Shift coverage

Coverage that stays on track.

Shifts are planned and tracked automatically. Late starts, absences, and changes are caught the moment they happen—so your posts stay filled and nothing slips by unnoticed.

03 · Post visibility

Review locations and assigned guards.

The Locations view brings together assigned guard counts, mapping and operating status. Search for a location to review its details.

04 · Incident visibility

Reports the moment they happen.

When something comes up, your guard reports it on the spot—with photos, time, and location attached. You get a clear record as events unfold, not a summary days later.

Photos · time · location · incident record

05 · One workspace

Everything on one screen.

See your guards, check-ins, reports, and coverage on one simple dashboard—from your phone or your desk, any time.
