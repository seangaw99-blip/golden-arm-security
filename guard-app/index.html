Install the Golden Arm app on your Android phone to stay connected with your team, get real-time updates, and manage your shifts on the go.

How to install

  1. Download — Tap Download App to save the file to your Android phone.
  2. Open the file — Open it from your notification panel, or from your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install — If asked, allow installs from unknown sources in your settings.
  4. Install — Tap Install and wait for it to finish.
  5. Sign in — Open the app and sign in. Need access? Contact your administrator.
