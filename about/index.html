Who We Are

Who we are

Golden Arm is a licensed security agency in the Philippines. We guard business sites for BPOs, hotels, banks, and warehouses, with guards trained for each site. We plan the posts, check every shift, and send you clear reports. We are based in Metro Manila, with regional offices in the Visayas and Mindanao.

Motto, Vision, Mission
